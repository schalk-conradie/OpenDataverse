# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Dynamics 365 developers on macOS. They are technical, time-constrained, and spend most of their day in IDEs, terminals, and browser tabs. They use OpenDataverse to do work that is awkward in the browser: register and debug plugins, sync web resources, build FetchXML, explore solutions, and ask an AI about their environment.

## Product Purpose

OpenDataverse is a native-feeling desktop workbench for Microsoft Dataverse and Dynamics 365 workflows. It owns Dataverse auth locally, keeps tokens out of the browser, and gives developers a focused, keyboard-friendly workspace for the tasks that browser-based tooling makes tedious.

## Brand Personality

Beautiful, minimal, clean.

The interface should feel like a precision tool: calm enough to live in all day, confident enough to trust with production work. No marketing theater, no visual noise. Every surface should look considered and behave predictably.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS dashboard aesthetics: gradient hero cards, big metrics, decorative illustrations.
- Bloated enterprise tools: dense toolbars, overlapping side panels, visual clutter.
- AI-generated scaffolding: identical card grids, tiny uppercase eyebrows, numbered section markers, side-stripe borders, gradient text.
- Over-decorated components: glassmorphism, bouncing motion, superfluous shadows.

## Design Principles

1. **Practice what you preach.** The app is built for developers who value craft; the UI should look and feel crafted.
2. **Disappear into the task.** Tools, not toys. The interface gets out of the way so the developer can focus on their environment.
3. **Clarity through restraint.** One type family, one accent role, consistent spacing. Prefer fewer, better decisions.
4. **Calm confidence.** Stable layouts, predictable motion, readable contrast. Nothing should feel fragile or accidental.
5. **Respect the platform.** macOS-native sensibilities: subtle depth, clean edges, sensible shortcuts, and a dark mode that feels at home.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA target. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. Ensure keyboard focus states are visible and color is not the sole indicator of state.
